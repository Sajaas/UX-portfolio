Project Demigod

As part of the publishing team at Fast Travel Games, I conducted UX testing for our publishing title Project Demigod, a physics based superhero simulator.

I focused on improving the tutorial and onboarding experience through testing and design discussions with the team. I identified and suggested solutions to various issues including unclear ability wheel introduction and unclear prompts.

IDENTIFYING THE ISSUES

I conducted a usability playtest on the tutorial to make sure player got the information they needed to start playing, and provided the developer with the top insights and suggested solutions.

Players also thought the tutorial had a good lenght, and they had no issue navigating through it. They could also start a game afterwards, and feel like they had an idea on how to approach the game.

While player enjoyed the superhero fantasy, and were impressed with the abilities, there were some issues regarding owerhelming information in the tutorial.

Top insights included:

  • The current open/close for the notification system was clunky and non-intuative
  • The texts and prompts were sometimes too long and unclear
  • The ability wheel and the sub abilities were still unclear
  • It was unclear that you can activate abilities on both hands
  • Menus were missing standardized language and tooltips
  • Ability wheel icons were unclear
SUGGESTED SOLUTIONS

Possible solutions included:

  • Redesign the notification system so it's still visible if players close the prompts
  • Remove or make the need to double tap to open/close notifications optional
  • Run the instructions and prompts by someone who is new to the game (suggestions included)
  • Introduce the ability sets visually
  • Standardize the menu language and add tooltips with explanations
  • Choose clearer icons for abilities (suggestions were included)
KEY TAKEAWAYS
  • Out of sight, out of mind - keep information available
  • Use standardized language and icons
  • Utilize tooltips
  • Always let someone new to the game read instructions
  • Update goals as players succeed
