Vampire the Masquerade: Justice

I conducted multiple UX tests, had design discussions, and research projects for our title Vampire the Masquerade: Justice, a first person single player adventure RPG set in the World of Darkness.

I also conduced research about accessibility and comfort options in VR, and presented a guide with prioritized features.

CROSSBOW FEATURE TEST, ONBOARDING USABILITY, PLAYER STEALTH BEHAVIORS

I conducted a feature usability tests of the crossbow, as well as a tutorial & onboarding usability playtest. I also did a follow up with the same testers, and analyzed the stealth features and player behaviours.

Top insights and suggestions from the testing include:

  • Lacking feedback for actions confuse players
  • Hunger concept is hard to grasp
  • Unclear when you can suck blood
  • Overwhelming tutorial
  • AI acts unexpectedly
  • Not punishing enough to play heads-on
  • Players tried to use Long Reach ability (force grab) on rats
SUGGESTED SOLUTIONS
  • Add more feedback for actions
  • Add controller button indications to tutorial
  • Repeat important actions and information
  • Clarify hunger and the icon related to it
  • While the violent approach felt unsatisfying, it was also easy to choose. Make it harder to be heads-on
  • Allow players to force grab rats
KEY TAKEAWAYS
  • Feedback to actions is key to understanding the game
  • VR benefits from contextual reminders and hints
  • Multiple prompts and long texts are not good in VR
  • Iconography is important
  • Players will choose the "easy way" even if it's not satisfying
