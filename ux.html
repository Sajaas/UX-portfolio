User Experience Research & Design

UX Research & Project Management at Fast Travel Games

As the sole UX Researcher at Fast Travel Games, I was responsible for designing, executing, and managing the entire user research (UXR) process across multiple projects. My role encompassed everything from introducing UX research to external development teams, booking and prioritizing research projects, designing and conducting playtests, and delivering actionable reports and presentations. I ensured that each project received proper UX testing, balancing priorities when schedules collided and aligning research efforts with development milestones.

End-to-End UX Research Management
  • Solely responsible for the entire UXR process, from onboarding external teams to playtesting execution and reporting.
  • Managed project scheduling, ensuring all critical games received testing while prioritizing efforts based on project importance and development needs.
  • Introduced UX best practices to teams unfamiliar with structured user research, integrating UXR into development workflows.
Participant Recruitment & Playtest Execution
  • Built and maintained an external player database, recruiting participants worldwide through social media, Reddit, Discord forums, and word of mouth.
  • Designed and launched a playtest signup and FAQ website, ensuring visibility by linking it to the company site.
  • Established user journeys, user tagging, automations, and campaign structures in MailChimp to streamline recruitment and communication.
  • Handled project-specific signups and feedback collection, initially through Google Forms and later transitioning to SurveyMonkey for improved data tracking and analysis.
  • Conducted playtesting through a variety of formats, including moderated in-person, remote, and unmoderated usability tests.
Data Collection, Analysis & Reporting
  • Delivered detailed UXR reports that included player insights, prioritized issues, and actionable suggestions.
  • Provided mockups and design recommendations to address usability pain points and improve game experiences.
  • Created "Common UX Pitfalls" documentation, identifying recurring issues across multiple research projects and providing new teams with preemptive UX guidelines.
  • Played and evaluated games before conducting playtests, helping teams avoid common usability mistakes before user testing.
  • Adjusted UX best practices based on target audiences and the evolving VR industry, ensuring research insights remained relevant to the rapidly changing landscape.
Cross-Team Collaboration & Communication
  • Translated research findings into clear, prioritized recommendations, ensuring insights were actionable within production constraints.
  • Delivered presentations and reports to developers, designers, and external partners, aligning teams on UX improvements.
  • Maintained communication with external studios, ensuring research results were effectively integrated into their development processes.
Process Development & Efficiency Improvements
  • Standardized UX research workflows, templates, and documentation for efficiency and consistency across projects.
  • Developed playtesting best practices tailored to VR and non-VR games, addressing unique interaction challenges.
  • Continuously refined UX methodologies to fit different development cadences, from early concept validation to final usability testing.
Project management leadership with a Team of UX Designers

In addition to independently managing UX research, I have experience leading teams of UX designers, ensuring structured workflows, clear communication, and impactful research outcomes. I coordinated research efforts, assigned tasks, managed project timelines, and aligned our findings with development teams to implement meaningful UX improvements.

  • Managed fast-paced UX iteration for Madshot, refining UI readability, player feedback systems, and usability while prioritizing quick, high-impact improvements.
  • Acted as the primary stakeholder liaison, ensuring UX insights were effectively communicated and implemented by developers.
  • Designed and executed user personas, usability testing, and feedback loops, tailoring recommendations to diverse player types.
  • Structured documentation, research workflows, and team collaboration strategies to streamline processes and improve efficiency.
  • Balanced tight deadlines and limited implementation time by prioritizing the most impactful UX improvements.
